Work with me

Get stronger for what matters to you.

My work takes different forms, but the starting point is always the woman in front of me. Whether you are here to get stronger, build your fitness career, bring meaningful education to a group, or explore a collaboration, we begin with what matters to you.

ONE-TO-ONE COACHING

One-to-One Strength Coaching

One-to-one coaching is especially suited to women returning to strength training after injury or surgery, or working with limitations that require closer attention.

The individual format gives us the time to focus on execution, make precise adjustments, and rebuild strength progressively. Where appropriate, I work alongside your physiotherapist or healthcare provider so your training supports the work already being done.

Client stories

What changed through training.

These women started in different places. What connects them is measurable progress in training and practical changes in daily life.

Strength after major surgery“

After two C-sections and major abdominal surgery, I’d lost all my core strength. Within six months I was strength training, going on vacation and doing hard things, keeping up with my kids, even training while fasting through Ramadan. She knew how I ate as a South Asian woman and coached me around my real life. It was her warmth and encouragement that changed my life for the better.

Anam S.Client, 6 years
From bodyweight squat to a 125 lb barbell“

I went from not being able to do a proper bodyweight squat to squatting a 125 lb barbell, both a physical and a highly emotional victory. From the time I started, I lost 30 pounds and went down almost three dress sizes. But more than that, I signed up for a trainer and got a world-class mentor, a movement specialist, and an extraordinary coach.

Maryam H.Client, 6 years
A plan that moves with real life“

I’d never been under a bar in over a decade of training with other trainers. Salwa moulds the entire plan around whatever is happening in your life. You never feel like you’re failing; you always feel on track. She’s a life coach, trainer, and guide, not just the hour you see her at the gym.

Mehwish J.Client, 5 years
Rebuilding strength after injury“

After a fall left me with a compression fracture, I thought I’d have to live with the limits. She built a progressive program with my physio and understood my body mechanics exceptionally well. It has literally been life-changing. I can resume and enjoy so many of the daily activities I couldn’t after the accident.
