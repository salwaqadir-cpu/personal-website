ONE-TO-ONE COACHING

One-to-One Strength Coaching

Mississauga and Milton, Ontario

One-to-one coaching is especially suited to women returning to strength training after injury or surgery, or working with limitations that require closer attention.

The individual format gives us the time to focus on execution, make precise adjustments, and rebuild strength progressively. Where appropriate, I work alongside your physiotherapist or healthcare provider so your training supports the work already being done.

The Women’s
Barbell Club

Intention • Strength • Sisterhood

Lighten Your Life.Build strength. Fuel your body. Navigate real life together.

I founded The Women’s Barbell Club to bring my Train With Efficiency approach into a community where women can build strength, understand their training, and stay supported as real life changes.

WBC is more than women lifting. It is a hybrid online and in-person community bringing together strength programming, technique, workout classes, lifting events, meal prep and fuel, retreats, and the sisterhood that helps carry the load of life.
