Women’s Strength Coach · Educator · Athlete · FOUNDER

Train with efficiency.

While the world is asking women to do more, I show you how to do less, so you can do more.

You can do a bodyweight squat or barbell squat. You can squat in a way where you feel more in your knees, or in your thighs, or in your glutes or in your calves.

None of these are wrong.

The question is: what do you want, why do you want it and how do you get there?

Train With Efficiency shows you how these variations differ, how your goals determine which one we choose, and what you should feel when you're doing it.

Because in this day and age, who has time to do more?

Lighten Your Life.

I founded The Women’s Barbell Club to bring my Train With Efficiency approach into a community where women can build strength, understand their training, and stay supported as life changes.

WBC is more than women lifting.

It is a hybrid online and in-person community bringing together efficient strength technique classes, programs, workout classes, lifting events, meal prep, recovery elements, retreats, and the sisterhood that helps you carry the load of life, for you to do less.
